# Space Lens

Space Lens is an offline visual review of local storage usage.

## What it shows

The view helps identify large folders and files by presenting scan results as a storage map and list. The sizes are local observations and may depend on permissions, filesystem metadata, and the scan boundary.

## How to use it

1. Open **Space Lens** from the sidebar, or from the Space Lens tab in Disk Cleaner. Home's **Large Files** opens it with the size filter set to items over 100 MB; the start screen says so before you scan.
2. Scan a permitted folder.
3. Use the map and list to understand which areas occupy the most apparent space.
4. Reveal a location when you need to inspect it in Finder, with the folder button at the start of its row (the same Show in Finder icon other pages use).
5. Select **Open in Experience** when you want eligible cache items reviewed for a move to the Trash. Like the other read-only lists, Space Lens ends with this card after a scan; the sidebar section and the Disk Cleaner tab show the same Space Lens, with the same scan, filters and card.

The list is the keyboard and VoiceOver route through the same items as the map. Each row is a button that reads the item's name and size, and pressing a folder opens it. The details bar follows the focused row as well as the pointer. The bubble map itself is hidden from VoiceOver.

## What this page does not do

Space Lens visualizes and reveals storage evidence only. It does not remove files, move items to Trash, alter folders, or perform storage repair.

A scan can be cancelled and stops when you leave the screen. A scan that fails says why above the map. Very large folders are capped at 500,000 entries; the header says when results were cut short.
